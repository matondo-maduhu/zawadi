ORODHA YA MAFAILI YA KUPAKIA (kila moja liwe kwenye folda yake kama ilivyoandikwa)
======================================================================


FOLDA: (folda kuu ya repository)
   - Procfile   -> amri ya kuanzisha gunicorn
   - README.md   -> maelezo ya mradi
   - app.py   -> kuu: link za zawadi, majibu, tarehe maalum, PWA, masharti
   - db.py   -> hifadhidata (SQLite/Postgres) + majibu + tarehe maalum
   - requirements.txt   -> inaongeza psycopg2-binary

FOLDA: events
   - 11-mtoto.json   -> MPYA: tukio jipya
   - 12-mwalimu.json   -> MPYA: tukio jipya
   - 13-mbali.json   -> MPYA: tukio jipya

FOLDA: static
   - manifest.webmanifest   -> MPYA: manifest ya PWA

FOLDA: static/icons
   - apple-touch-icon.png   -> MPYA: ikoni ya app
   - favicon-32.png   -> MPYA: ikoni ya app
   - icon-192.png   -> MPYA: ikoni ya app
   - icon-512.png   -> MPYA: ikoni ya app
   - icon-maskable-512.png   -> MPYA: ikoni ya app

FOLDA: templates
   - 404.html   -> ukurasa wa haipatikani
   - create.html   -> kutengeneza: kurekodi sauti, tarehe, makubaliano, link
   - index.html   -> ukurasa wa mwanzo + kitufe cha kusakinisha
   - legal.html   -> MPYA: masharti na faragha
   - manage.html   -> ukurasa wa mmiliki: majibu na tarehe
   - offline.html   -> MPYA: ukurasa wa mtandao ukikatika
   - pwa_check.html   -> MPYA: ukaguzi wa PWA
   - sw.js   -> MPYA: service worker (PWA)
   - waiting.html   -> MPYA: kuhesabu muda kabla ya zawadi kufunguka

FOLDA: templates/gifts
   - book.html   -> mtindo wa kitabu (+ kisanduku cha majibu)
   - cards.html   -> mtindo wa kadi (+ kisanduku cha majibu)
   - phone.html   -> mtindo wa simu (+ kisanduku cha majibu)
